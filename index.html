Important storage information

Movies are saved in this browser on this device. They remain after closing and reopening the site, but they are not uploaded to an online server. Clearing browser data, using another browser/device, or running out of device storage can remove or prevent saving them. The 15 GB figure is an app limit; your browser/device may allow less.

Sharing this website link shares the page only—not the movie files. To let other people watch, you need private cloud video storage and a server/login system.

Use this library only for videos you own or have permission to store and share. Keep a separate backup of important files.
